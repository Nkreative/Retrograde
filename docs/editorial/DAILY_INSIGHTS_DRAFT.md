# Daily insights — editorial draft v1 (for review)

How a selected day's detail is assembled:

1. **Level + summary** — one line chosen from the level's pool (keyed to the day's strongest influence where a specific line exists).
2. **What's shaping the day** — one row per influence: name, small contribution tag, one-line explanation.
3. **Good habit for this day** — one practical habit from the level's pool.

Selection is deterministic (based on the date), so a day always reads the same; neighbouring days don't repeat the same line. Voice: playful, reassuring, practical; never fatalistic; astrology as a reflection prompt.

---

## 1. Summaries by level

### Calm (0)
- Nothing loud in the sky today. A good day to do the thing you've been circling.
- Quiet orbit. Make plans, send the email, book the table.
- Clear-ish skies. Use the calm to get a little ahead.
- A low-noise day. Your future self would love a small head start.

### Mild (1–2)
- A little background hum. Nothing a second read-through can't handle.
- Slightly busier sky. Leave a few minutes of slack and you're fine.
- *(shadow lead)* Mercury's still in its shadow. Tie up loose ends while things are steady.
- *(moon lead)* A New/Full Moon adds a little energy. Notice it; you don't have to act on it.

### Elevated (3–4)
- A little louder, not a lost cause. Slow down where details matter.
- *(Mercury Rx lead)* Review-mode Mercury. Revisit before you send, and leave room for replies to wander.
- Several things stacking up. Build in buffers and keep plans simple.
- Busy sky, steady you. Double-check, then let it go.

### Intense (5+)
- *(station lead)* Mercury changes direction today — the noisiest day of the cycle. Keep it gentle and keep it simple.
- *(station, turning direct)* Mercury turns direct today. Things start moving again, just not all at once. Give it a day.
- *(station, turning retrograde)* Mercury turns retrograde today. Back up, slow down, and read twice.
- Peak static. Lower the stakes where you can; important things can wait a day.

## 3. Good habits by level

**Calm**
- Send the message you've been drafting.
- Book the appointment you keep postponing.
- Do one 10-minute tech chore: updates or a backup.
- Plan one thing for later this week.

**Mild**
- Read important messages once more before sending.
- Add 10 minutes to anything time-sensitive today.
- Confirm one plan with the person involved.
- Close one loose loop from earlier this week.

**Elevated**
- Save the draft. Read it out loud, check what you actually mean, then send.
- Confirm time, place and booking before you leave.
- Back up the one file you'd miss most.
- Pick one nonessential plan to move, and say so kindly.

**Intense**
- Lower the stakes: postpone what can wait a day.
- Pause for one slow breath before replying to anything that stings.
- Keep plans simple and leave an extra buffer.
- Write down what's in your control right now. Start there.
